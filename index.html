About me

I am applying to PhD programs for Fall 2027 to research efficient and interpretable machine learning. I am a first-generation college student completing a double major in Computer Science and Mathematics at The University of Southern Mississippi (May 2027).

Since August 2026, I have been doing undergraduate research with Dr. Ahsan Ali on low-rank compression of large language models, supported by the Cross Mathematics Undergraduate Research Scholarship. I study how SVD-based methods can be modified or combined to shrink transformer models while keeping their accuracy, and why a small error in the weights can still change what the model computes. Before this, I completed the Computing Research Association's UR2PhD program on how to do computing research.

In graduate school, I want to build the mathematical foundations for making large models smaller, faster and easier to understand, bringing together numerical linear algebra, deep learning, model compression and interpretability. My long-term goal is a career as a research scientist in academia or an industry lab.
