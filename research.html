Low-Rank Compression of Language Models

Aug 2026 – present
Advisor: Dr. Ahsan Ali · School of Mathematics and Natural Sciences, USM

Studying how SVD-based techniques can be modified or combined to compress transformer language models while keeping their accuracy. I reproduce truncated SVD and SVD-LLM's activation-aware whitened truncation on DistilGPT2, and compare how well each preserves the weights versus the layer outputs ‖WX − W′X‖F.

Why: large language models are expensive to store and run. Weight matrices often have structure that low-rank approximation can exploit, and the open question is when a small error in the weights still leads to a large error in what the model computes.

QR-Code Phishing (“Quishing”) Detection

Jun – Aug 2026
Team project · CRA Undergraduate Research to PhD (UR2PhD) program

Trained a convolutional neural network in PyTorch to tell benign QR codes from malicious ones using only their visual pattern, on a balanced dataset of 9,987 QR images. Our team also evaluated Vision Transformer classifiers.

Why: attackers increasingly hide phishing links inside QR codes, which people scan without seeing the URL. Detecting a malicious code before it is opened is a useful last line of defense.
