Companion: a fully offline voice AI agent

2026

A privacy-first voice assistant for Linux that runs entirely on local hardware: Whisper for speech-to-text, a local LLM through Ollama, and Piper for speech. It can search files, read web pages and control the mouse and keyboard, but asks for spoken confirmation before any action and verifies the speaker's voice first.

Why: most voice assistants send what you say to the cloud. Companion explores how capable an agent can be with no data leaving the machine, and how to give an LLM real control of a computer safely.

CoDrive: shared-car trips and costs

2026

A web app for people who share a car: record trips by odometer, split estimated fuel costs among riders, log expenses and payments, and see who owes what. It includes Google Maps routes, invitation codes for co-owners, and Gemini-powered reading of odometer photos and fuel receipts.

Why: splitting the cost of a shared car fairly is surprisingly messy without a record of who drove, how far and who paid.

Electricity demand forecasting

2026
AI4ALL team project (Group 11A)

Forecasting electricity demand one day, one week and one month ahead from U.S. Energy Information Administration data merged with weather and lagged-demand features. We compared linear regression, Random Forest and XGBoost, and served the results in a Streamlit dashboard.

Why: grid operators need to know demand ahead of time to balance supply, especially as more power comes from weather-dependent solar and wind.
